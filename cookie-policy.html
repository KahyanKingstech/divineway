1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as localStorage (browser storage) to save preferences like your shopping cart and login state between visits.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use only cookies that are necessary for the site to function. We do not use advertising, tracking, or profiling cookies.

3. Strictly Necessary Cookies & Storage

These cannot be disabled as they are essential for the site to operate.

4. No Analytics or Advertising Cookies

We do not currently use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any third-party advertising or behavioural tracking cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated and your consent sought before any such cookies are set.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent core features (login, cart) from working. Instructions for common browsers:
