1. Introduction

DivinewayFengshui ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). This policy explains what data we collect when you use our website and services, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and profile photo obtained via Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication).
  • Order and payment data: Items purchased, order amounts, and Stripe Checkout session IDs. Full card details are processed by Stripe and never stored on our systems.
  • Technical data: Browser type, device information, and approximate location collected automatically by Cloudflare (our hosting infrastructure).
  • Communications: Messages or enquiries you send via WhatsApp or our contact channels.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To process and fulfil your orders and send you order confirmation emails.
  • To create and maintain your customer account in our backend system (ERPNext).
  • To respond to enquiries and provide after-sales support.
  • To comply with legal obligations (tax records, invoicing).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party processors who may handle your data:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication and order history storage (Google Privacy Policy applies).
  • Stripe — Secure payment processing (Stripe Privacy Policy applies).
  • Cloudflare — Website delivery and security (Cloudflare Privacy Policy applies).
  • ERPNext / Frappe Cloud — Business operations and order management.
  • Resend — Transactional email delivery for order confirmations.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session and preserve your shopping cart. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

6. Data Retention

Account and order data is retained for as long as your account is active and for up to 7 years thereafter for accounting and legal compliance. You may request deletion of non-legally-required data at any time.

7. Your Rights Under the PDPA

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw consent and request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations).

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, or destruction. Payment data is handled exclusively by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS certified.
