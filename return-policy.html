Each talisman is personally hand-drawn and ritually consecrated by Master Louis for your specific intention. Because of this bespoke, spiritual nature, our return policy differs from standard retail. Please read this policy carefully before placing an order.

1. Talismans & Consecrated Items

Due to the custom and personalised nature of our talismans — each one drawn by hand and consecrated through Taoist ritual specifically for the buyer — we are unable to accept returns or issue refunds once the consecration process has begun.

Consecration typically commences within 1–3 business days of payment confirmation. If you need to cancel your order, please contact us immediately via WhatsApp at +65 8949 0437.

2. Cancellation Before Consecration

If you contact us within 24 hours of payment and before the consecration process has started, we will issue a full refund to your original payment method. Refunds are processed via Stripe and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

To request a cancellation, WhatsApp us with your order reference number (visible in your order confirmation email).

3. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your item arrives damaged in transit or you receive the wrong item, please:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery via WhatsApp.
  • Include your order reference and clear photographs of the item and packaging.

We will arrange a replacement or full refund at our discretion, including any applicable shipping costs.

4. Divine Items & Physical Products

Sealed or opened physical products (incense, amulets, spiritual tools) may be returned within 7 days if unused and in original packaging. Return shipping costs are borne by the buyer unless the item was incorrect or damaged.

5. Consultation & Advisory Services

BaZi Analysis, Feng Shui Audit, Name Analysis, and other advisory services are non-refundable once the consultation session has commenced or the report has been delivered. Rescheduling requests must be made at least 24 hours in advance.

6. Academy Courses

Course enrolments are non-refundable once access to course materials has been granted. If you are unable to attend a live session, a recording will be made available where possible.

7. How to Contact Us

For all return, refund, or cancellation requests:

  • WhatsApp: +65 8949 0437 (Mon – Sat, 10am – 7pm SGT)

Please have your order reference number ready. We aim to respond to all queries within one business day.
