The teachings of Divine Magic （六壬仙法）

Divine Magic

Liu Ren Divine Magic 六壬仙法 is a simple and convenient skill to master. In the past, people studied this ancient art mainly for self protection. As times have changed, students who learn these days usually do it mainly to help attune their energies to improve their Careers, People Affinity, Wealth Luck and Health.

Students need to provide their name and Date of Birth. The master will then write a letter and hold a ritual to invite the gods to witness the initiation ceremony.

After which the letter is burnt to be reported to the gods. The students will then drink a bowl of talisman water which will help them ward of evil and bring about good luck. After which a ceremony will be held to ‘seal’ the student’s body using magical spells and chants. This is finalized by affixing a seal. After all this is done, the initiation process will be held (Also know as the crossing of Sect). The student will receive the divine energy and will also be taught a magical verse to use when communicating with the divine.

Grand Master Lee Chun Feng(六壬仙师 李淳風) is the founder of Taoist Sect the Liu Ren Sect 六壬法门. He passed on his teachings in the form of Mediation, Talisman Drawing, Rituals, and Chants with the goal of aiding people in need.

We welcome new students who are interested to learn Liu Ren Divine Magic(六壬仙法).

Different Levels of Learning

Level 1 (Middle Sect)

Includes a Small Sect which is mainly for self protection, warding off evil. Students will also learn some basic spells to stop bleeding, dissolve bones, treat scalding, ward evil, treat headaches etc.

Level 2 (Big Sect)

Students will learn how to cultivate their energy and learn more advance spells and chants.

Level 3 (3 Mountain Sect)

Students will learn to draw talismans and increase their positive energy.

Level 4 (5 Thunder Sect)

Students will be able to learn to draw more advanced talismans and spells. They will also learn skills to help themselves and others ward off evil, danger and preparators and also relieve health problems. They will also be able to learn chants and spells to aid them in improving their People Affinity and Wealth.

After this stage, students might also wish to take up specialized courses in specific fields of interest which include Rituals related to Wealth, Health, Relationships, etc. Upon completion of mastering all the specialized courses, students with further interests might raise the desire of being a Teacher to guide and accept students of their own themselves to the Master.

(Without the Master’s official decree and approval, disciples who accept students are unqualified and their students are not considered official students of the Liu Ren Sect Lineage). Please check if the person you are learning from has a qualified approval from his/her master to teach as it will do more harm than good learning under an unqualified master).

Criteria to learn

This is a skill that has no restrains to gender, age, occupation, race, nationality, religious background, and prior knowledge. Students only need to have a sincere heart and strong belief.

There are no Taboos!

There are no restrictions of any sort and after becoming a student it can improve your general luck, help to ward of danger and prevent black magic. There are no side effects if you have crossed under a qualified master who has bestowed real teachings to you.

There is a secret password to every level and students have to memorize it by hard. This code is chanted whenever the student wants to invoke any spells or chants.

**Please note that the password should not be recited out loud nor should it be disclosed to anyone else. As such students have to respect and adhere to this tradition as a rule.**
